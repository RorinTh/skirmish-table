Terrain

Click a piece to place it in the middle of the table. Colours show its Size: scatter, low cover, sight-blocking, high ground, or grass (blocks sight but can be crossed). The rulebook suggests 8–12 pieces on a 54×36″ table.

Custom piece

Select a piece and press Q / E to turn it. Inches.

Save & load

The board is kept in this browser automatically. To move a game elsewhere, copy the game state and paste it on the other side.
